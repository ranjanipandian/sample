🎯 About PreMortem

PreMortem is an adversarial decision-governance framework built natively 
on Cognizant's Neuro SAN platform.

The core idea is simple but powerful:

Most AI systems audit the data.
PreMortem audits the DECISION.

Instead of a single AI agent recommending an answer, PreMortem runs a 
structured tribunal where specialized agents are incentivized to 
challenge, defend, and audit every aspect of a high-stakes decision.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚖️ How the Tribunal Works

🎭 5 Prosecutors (in parallel)
Biology · Safety · Precedent · Translational · Tractability
Each files evidence-backed charges from their domain.

🛡️ Defence Advocate
Responds to every charge — rebutting or conceding, point by point.

⚖️ Arbiter (Python — not LLM)
Calculates a deterministic risk score using a published rubric.
Same inputs → Same output, every time.

🔍 Devil's Advocate
Runs 8 deterministic checks on the reasoning chain itself.
If the reasoning is flawed → the tribunal retries. (Max 2 rounds)

📋 Clerk
Produces the Executive Risk Dossier:
Verdict · Risk Score · Citation Ledger · Dissent Report

💡 Why This Matters

For decisions costing hundreds of millions — drug development, 
investments, architecture — "the agents agreed" is not governance.

PreMortem delivers:
→ Auditable decisions with full citations
→ Transparent reasoning chains
→ Governance over consensus
→ Dissent published, not hidden

The framework is domain-agnostic. What we demonstrated on drug-target 
evaluation can be applied to any high-stakes decision.
